KYC and AML Procedure (NW-CMP-KYC-01). Version 4.1, effective 1 January 2025. Owner: Compliance.

1. Purpose and scope. Northwind Capital expects consistent practice on customer due diligence and money laundering. This document describes that practice, who is responsible for it and what to do when something does not fit. It is reviewed by its owner on a fixed cycle.

2. Definitions. In this document an employee means anyone on the company payroll, a manager means a line manager at grade G3 or above, and a working day means any day that is not a Saturday, a Sunday or a company holiday. The owner is the function named in the review section.

3. Onboarding. Identity must be verified before an account is opened. Politically exposed persons need enhanced due diligence on form KYC-EDD-2. Customer details are refreshed every year for high-risk customers, every 3 years for medium-risk customers and every 5 years for low-risk customers.

4. Reporting. Transactions above INR 10 lakh are reported to the monitoring team. Suspicious activity is escalated to the MLRO within 24 hours, and a suspicious transaction report is filed within 7 days of the decision.

5. Roles and responsibilities. The owner keeps this document up to date and answers questions about it. Line managers make sure their teams know about it and apply it. Everyone else is responsible for reading it and following it, and for raising anything that looks wrong with their manager or the owner.

6. Exceptions and escalation. If a case does not fit, ask Compliance before acting. An approved exception is written down, time limited and reviewed. Repeated exceptions on the same point are taken as a sign that the document needs changing.

7. Review and ownership. This document is owned by Compliance and reviewed every year. Questions go to the owner. Related documents: Sanctions Screening Standard; Data Retention Schedule.
